Removing friction from account opening

I prioritized more than 15 A/B tests across Toss Bank's account-opening journey. Acquisition conversion improved by 15%.

Find where people stopped

Traffic was growing, but people were dropping out between the landing page and a completed account. I used heatmaps, event logs, and interviews to investigate unclear product copy, long forms, and confusing instructions.

Keep experiments small enough to ship

I worked with designers and engineers to take an idea from review to a live A/B test in one to three days. We tested onboarding copy, page hierarchy, input-field order, and loading transitions.

Dashboards tracked conversion during each test. We shared findings weekly so acquisition and marketing teams could reuse changes that worked.

Result

Across the experiments, acquisition conversion improved by 15%.
