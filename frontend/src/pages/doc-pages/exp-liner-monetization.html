Helping developers evaluate and pay for Liner

I owned growth and monetization for Liner's AI search API during my MBA internship, May to August 2026. The redesigned purchase journey tripled landing-to-paid conversion.

Make the product easier to evaluate

Liner offered web retrieval, cited answers, quick answers, and longer research reports. Developers needed to understand which API fit their product and whether its quality justified the cost.

I added interactive product comparisons to the acquisition journey. Developers could compare outputs while evaluating Liner. The work also included benchmark reports and charts that put answer quality beside cost per request.

I evaluated search retrieval separately from generated answers and kept test conditions and pricing assumptions visible in the benchmark materials.

Connect the trial to paid usage

I paired the comparison experience with bonus API credits for adding a payment card. Developers had more room to test the API, and a payment method was already in place when they wanted to keep using it.

These changes addressed two steps in the same journey: deciding whether the API was useful, then setting up payment.

Result

Across the comparison experience and card incentive together, landing-to-paid conversion increased 3x.
