Different Joint Accounts for couples and clubs

I used behavioral analysis of more than one million users to tailor the Joint Account to how different groups managed money. Daily account openings rose 30%, and NPS rose 20 points.

One account served different needs

Couples, partners, and activity groups all received the same product. Their reasons for sharing an account differed, but the landing page and features did little to reflect that.

Choose differences worth building for

I used SQL and Python to examine usage patterns and grouped customers by behavior. We focused on groups whose needs differed enough to justify changes, starting with couples and clubs.

We tailored landing pages and features, including budgeting for couples and shared asset tracking for clubs. Reusable components let us adapt the experience without maintaining separate products. Interviews and feedback sessions helped us check the designs.

Result

Daily Joint Account openings increased 30%, and net promoter score increased 20 points.
