Sharing that worked between existing customers

I built a reusable sharing system that let customers send seasonal greetings and receive small rewards, whether or not the recipient already used Toss Bank.

Referral programs ran out of new friends

Toss Bank had passed six million customers. A referral reward that required a new signup became harder to use as more of a customer's friends already had accounts.

Make the invitation worth sending

We let members share with both members and non-members, with repeatable rewards of less than one cent per share. Campaigns used occasions such as Chuseok and New Year's greetings, with message templates customers could personalize.

We ran more than ten campaigns in six months. I helped turn the sharing flow into a reusable module, with documentation that other squads could use for savings and foreign-currency products.

Result

The Chuseok campaign generated 100M+ link shares in 72 hours and attracted 200K new users in three days.

After other product teams adopted the system, it brought in 3M+ additional users, about 40% of the bank's customer base at the time.
