A savings account built around a morning habit

Challenge Account asked people to join a daily wake-up challenge. It reached 300K+ monthly active users within a month, with more than 70% week-one retention.

The original idea hit a constraint

After Toss Bank's early acquisition growth, our Social Squad focused on giving customers a reason to return. We chose a familiar problem: getting up early.

The first concept pooled deposits and redistributed money to people who completed the challenge. Legal and Compliance ruled out that structure because participants could lose principal.

Reward participation while protecting deposits

I worked with Legal and Compliance to redesign the rewards around accumulated interest. Participants kept their principal, and the interest funded lottery-style prizes.

When participation slowed after the initial launch, we added prize tiers from first through fifth place so more participants could win. We also added a morning chat room that opened only to early participants.

Result

The product reached 300K+ MAU in its first month and 70%+ week-one retention. Company-wide churn fell by 5 percentage points.
