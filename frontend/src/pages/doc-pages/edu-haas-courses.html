Why I came to Haas

After working on live streaming and banking products, I wanted to understand AI more deeply and learn what it takes to start a company.

Agentic AI

I took this computer science course to understand how agents are built. As a PM, I wanted enough implementation experience to judge what a product could realistically do.

The course covered LLM foundations, reasoning, planning, agent infrastructure, and applications such as coding and web automation. It also examined where current agents fail.

AI Entrepreneurship

I had launched products inside companies, including Challenge Account at Toss Bank, but had never started a company from scratch. I took this course to practice building an early-stage venture.

The Ethics of AI

I joined this philosophy course to discuss questions I had been writing about on my blog, including AI safety and companionship. Topics included bias, privacy, surveillance, hallucinations, manipulation, and education.
