Researching 1,200 prospective accounts

I built an AI-assisted prospect research and outbound preparation system. The work contributed to an enterprise win and $400K in qualified annual recurring revenue pipeline.

Start with a reason to contact each company

For enterprise outreach, a company name and an email address were not enough. We needed to understand where a company used AI, which workload might fit Liner, and what evidence supported that fit.

I built the account workflow around those questions. Company records brought together use cases, source links, and a proposed reason to reach out. AI-assisted research helped prepare that context across 1,200 accounts.

Turn research into outreach

The GTM tool let me rank accounts, filter them into groups with similar needs, and prepare messaging for each group. The ranking included product fit and the strength of the supporting evidence, so I could inspect why an account appeared near the top.

Messaging and approval states sat alongside the account research. The workflow also tracked outreach and follow-up status, keeping the research connected to what happened next.

Commercial outcome

The work contributed to one enterprise win and $400K in qualified ARR pipeline. The pipeline figure describes potential annual recurring revenue from qualified opportunities. It is separate from booked revenue.
