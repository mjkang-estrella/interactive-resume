Trying productivity tools

I love productivity tools. I've tried over ten note-taking apps, including Notion, Obsidian, Capacities, Anytype, and Mem.ai. Some of the tools I've used:

  • Notion: My main note-taking tool
  • Anytype: Similar to Notion, but privacy-first, so I use it for personal knowledge management
  • Hey: Email app; I wanted an @hey.com address because it's cool!
  • Superhuman: Email app for fast communication at work
  • Dia: AI-first browser from The Browser Company
  • Kagi: Paid search engine replacing Google
  • Raycast: Smarter Spotlight for Mac
  • Monologue: Voice-based keyboard replacement
  • Motion: Personal project & task management tool
  • Reader: Readwise's newsletter reader. I love newsletters!
  • Ghost: My blogging platform
  • Zed: My go-to IDE, partly because I love Rust!
