Keeping the squad working toward the same goals

I introduced shared quarterly goals and regular feedback in a squad of six to eight people. Feature delivery became 20% faster, and team satisfaction reached 95%.

Give each discipline the same context

The squad included design, engineering, banking operations, and data analysis. We worked autonomously, so decisions depended on everyone understanding the product priorities and dependencies.

Make feedback a routine

Every four weeks, I held one-on-one coffee chats to discuss workload, product direction, and dependencies. Quarterly OKRs connected the squad's goals to company priorities.

Weekly meetings covered leadership updates and lessons from other squads. Designers, engineers, and analysts could bring questions about roadmap priorities into those discussions.

Result

Feature delivery was 20% faster, and team satisfaction reached 95%.
