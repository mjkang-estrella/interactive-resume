Growing accounts with a four-person team

As one of the four founding members of Toss Bank's Growth Squad, I worked with a designer and two engineers to bring in 2.1M+ new accounts in six months.

Grow without a large acquisition budget

The squad had to keep acquisition costs low while bringing in customers who would stay. We tracked cost per acquired user and week-one retention alongside account openings.

Build sharing into the product

We launched small cash rewards for account opening and built sharing and invitation flows into the app. Seasonal campaigns gave customers a reason to send account links and activities to friends.

I also worked with marketing on search and app-store discovery. We tested copy, incentive amounts, and the sharing experience, using cohort dashboards to follow retention and acquisition cost.

Result

The squad's work brought in 2.1M+ new accounts, a 30% increase in six months. Acquisition cost was about $3.50 per user, with week-one retention above 80%.

As the bank grew, the product focus shifted toward retention and engagement. I later joined the Social Squad to work on that problem.
